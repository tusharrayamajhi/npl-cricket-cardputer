# Credits

CardOS is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or
connected to the Nepal Premier League, its teams, ESPN or M5Stack.

## Match data

Scores, fixtures and results are read from ESPN's public cricket scoreboard
feed. It is an unofficial, undocumented feed and may change or stop at any time.

Team names, captains, coaches and squads come from the English Wikipedia
article [2026 Nepal Premier League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Nepal_Premier_League)
(text licensed [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)).

## Logos (`res/npl/`)

The Nepal Premier League logo and the eight team logos are trademarks of their
respective owners. They are included, resized for a 240x135 screen, only to
identify the league and teams in this fan app. If you are a rights holder and
want them removed, please open an issue.
